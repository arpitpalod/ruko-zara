1. Who we are

Ruko Zara ("we", "the app") is a free app where people ask strangers to vote on purchases before buying. It is run by Arpit Palod, an individual based in India. Contact: rukozara.app@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account: an account ID created automatically when you first open the app, and a random display name (for example "Kanjoos Kabootar 42").
  • Email address, only if you verify one. We use it to send login codes. It is never shown to other users.
  • What you post: requests (item, price, category, reason, cheaper option), your votes, comments, reports, blocks, and whether you finally bought the item.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider keeps server logs (IP address, time, browser or device type) for security and abuse prevention.
  • On your device only: your language choice and login session.

We do not collect your phone number, contacts, location, photos or payment details. We show no ads, use no advertising trackers, and never sell your data.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the app: show requests, count votes, show comments and your savings.
  • To log you in with a one-time code.
  • To keep the app safe: rate limits, reports, blocks, and removing content that breaks our Terms.
  • To comply with Indian law when legally required.

4. What other users can see

Your display name, your requests, your comments, and vote totals are visible to other users. How you voted is private: other users only ever see totals. Your email is never shown.

5. Where your data is stored and who processes it

  • Supabase: database and login. Servers in Mumbai, India.
  • Google (Gmail): sends login code emails.
  • GitHub: hosts this website and the Android download.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: a quick automatic check that you're a real person (not a bot) when you open the app or log in. It looks at technical signals from your browser or phone, not your name or email.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

  • We keep your data until you delete your account.
  • Delete in the app: My requests → Delete account. Your account, requests, votes and comments are deleted immediately and permanently.
  • Can't open the app? Email rukozara.app@gmail.com from your verified email address and we will delete it within 15 days.
  • Accounts that never verified an email and never posted are deleted automatically after 30 days.

7. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent by deleting your account. Write to the Grievance Officer below.

8. Age limit

Ruko Zara is only for people aged 18 or older. If we learn that a user is under 18, we delete the account.

9. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), and database rules ensure users can only change their own data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you.
